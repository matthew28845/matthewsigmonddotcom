<p>SpecMe is a free and open-source system specification viewer written in Electron. It will run on Windows, macOS, and Linux. SpecMe shows your computer's specifications such as processsor, memory, storage, graphics, and operating system. It also has dark mode!</p>
<p>SpecMe uses <a href="https://systeminformation.io/">systeminformation</a> by Sebastian Hildebrant to retrieve specs, and the <a href="https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/">Solarized</a> color scheme by Ethan Schoonover.</p>
